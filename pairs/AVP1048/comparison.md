## Rationales

On the left, the filled marker adds that coordinate pair to the graph and supplies the value at its horizontal coordinate. On the right, the hollow marker omits the indicated point and there is no other plotted value at x=2.

## Reasoning consequence

A solver can read f(1)=0 from the included point on the left and must leave x=2 outside the displayed domain on the right.
